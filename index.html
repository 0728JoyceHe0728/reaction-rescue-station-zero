    Lab guide

    Matter is never
    lost in space.

    Chemical equations are inventories: atoms are rearranged, not created or destroyed.

    01

    Count every atom

    Multiply the coefficient in front of a formula by the subscript of each element inside it.

    02

    Change only coefficients

    Coefficients adjust amounts. Changing a subscript would create a different chemical.

    03

    Use the smallest ratio

    A balanced equation can still be reduced. The standard form uses the smallest whole-number coefficients.
